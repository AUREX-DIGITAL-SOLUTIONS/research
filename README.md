# Aurex Digital Solutions — Research

This repository holds Aurex Digital Solutions' research projects. It is
maintained separately from the organization's institutional governance
repository, and is not itself a claim of institutional policy, partnership, or
product commitment.

## Purpose

Each project here investigates whether a specific, evidence-based problem
exists before Aurex commits resources to further research, prototyping, or
programme work. Projects follow a common discipline: separate fact from
inference, cite primary sources wherever possible, and record what would
change a conclusion.

## Working Rules

- Do not convert an assumption, interview statement, or promising technical
  idea into an institutional fact without recorded evidence.
- Prefer primary and official sources for material factual claims; note
  reliability, limitations, and conflicts of interest for every source.
- Do not generalize one facility, vendor, county, or year into a national
  claim without evidence.
- Keep each project's Source Register lean — retire unverified or low-quality
  leads rather than letting them accumulate as if they were confirmed.

## Structure

Each project folder is self-contained and typically includes:

- `README.md` — project overview and entry point
- `VALIDATION-PLAN.md` — research question, scope, method, decision gate
- `SOURCE-REGISTER.md` — verified primary/academic sources only
- `EVIDENCE-LOG.md` — findings, each tagged with an evidence class
- `ASSUMPTIONS-AND-RISKS.md` — assumption and risk registers
- `STAKEHOLDER-DISCOVERY.md` — interview guides and discovery log
- `DECISION-LOG.md` — material project decisions and their evidence
