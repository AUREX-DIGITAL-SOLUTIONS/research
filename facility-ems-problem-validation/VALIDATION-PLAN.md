# Facility EMS Problem Validation Plan

**Version:** 0.1
**Status:** Draft starter plan
**Project:** Facility EMS Problem Validation
**Geography:** Kenya, with context-specific findings only
**Owner:** Founder-led interim stewardship

## 1. Decision to Inform

Determine whether Aurex should define and fund a bounded Facility EMS research or prototype effort.

This plan must not assume that the answer is yes.

## 2. Research Question

> Where do meaningful visibility, interoperability, control, cost, reliability, or coordination gaps exist in Kenyan facility and distributed-energy contexts, what existing systems already address them, and what minimum useful Facility EMS capability is justified by evidence?

## 3. Current Hypotheses

Record each hypothesis in [Assumptions and Risks](ASSUMPTIONS-AND-RISKS.md). Initial hypotheses are deliberately unvalidated:

- Some facilities may operate heterogeneous energy resources with fragmented visibility.
- Existing systems may address parts of the problem but leave a material integration or coordination gap.
- A bounded Facility EMS capability may improve a measurable operational outcome in selected contexts.

## 4. Scope

### In scope

- facility energy-management workflows;
- solar, batteries, generators, loads, meters, EV charging, and related resources;
- existing monitoring, metering, BMS, EMS, SCADA, inverter, and vendor systems;
- interoperability and data availability;
- operational consequences and user value;
- authority, safety, security, privacy, and legal boundaries;
- minimum evidence needed for a prototype decision.

### Out of scope

- claiming a national visibility gap;
- designing a production control system;
- connecting to live assets without authorization;
- declaring a pilot or partnership;
- proving regional or national DERMS demand;
- collecting personal or operational data without a lawful basis and permission.

## 5. Evidence Streams

1. Primary and official sources.
2. Technical and academic literature.
3. Stakeholder discovery.
4. Existing-system and capability mapping.
5. Structured data or telemetry where authorized.
6. Controlled technical experiments only after the problem is sufficiently defined.

Record sources in [Source Register](SOURCE-REGISTER.md) and findings in [Evidence Log](EVIDENCE-LOG.md).

## 6. Stakeholder Categories

Use categories before naming organizations:

- facility owners and operators;
- energy engineers;
- DER installers and integrators;
- technology vendors;
- utilities and system operators;
- regulators and policy experts;
- researchers and universities;
- affected users and public-interest stakeholders.

Use [Stakeholder Discovery](STAKEHOLDER-DISCOVERY.md) for engagement records.

## 7. Method

For each research question, record:

- question and hypothesis;
- method and source selection;
- evidence needed;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- limitations and uncertainty;
- success and rejection criteria;
- decision affected.

## 8. Success Criteria

Validation may support prototype scoping only if evidence shows:

- a specific problem exists;
- identifiable stakeholders experience it;
- existing systems do not fully address the selected use case;
- the problem has a measurable operational, economic, reliability, or planning consequence;
- a bounded Facility EMS intervention is plausible;
- access, authority, safety, security, data, and legal conditions are understood;
- the proposed next step is proportionate to evidence and capacity.

## 9. Rejection or Pivot Criteria

The project should reject or revise the current thesis if:

- the problem is not evidenced;
- the issue is already adequately addressed;
- the problem is too broad to study responsibly;
- stakeholders do not experience material consequences;
- the proposed intervention lacks legitimate authority;
- the evidence does not justify technical work;
- safety, security, privacy, legal, or economic conditions are unacceptable.

## 10. Decision Gate

The decision should be recorded in [Decision Log](DECISION-LOG.md) as one of:

```text
Proceed to bounded prototype scoping
Continue research
Narrow or revise the use case
Pause
Reject current hypothesis
```
