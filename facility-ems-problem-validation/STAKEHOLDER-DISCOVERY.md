# Stakeholder Discovery

This record supports learning, not partnership claims.

## Discovery Rules

- Ask what problem exists before presenting a solution.
- Record the stakeholder category, role, and authority.
- Separate individual observations from validated patterns.
- Do not name an organization as a partner unless a documented relationship exists.
- Do not collect personal or sensitive information unnecessarily.

## Evidence Levels

- Individual observation
- Emerging pattern
- Supported evidence
- Validated finding

## Questions to Explore

- What energy resources and systems are present?
- What can the stakeholder currently see or control?
- What is fragmented, delayed, inaccessible, or unreliable?
- What operational consequence follows?
- What existing system or vendor already addresses part of the issue?
- Who owns the relevant data and assets?
- What permission would research or integration require?
- What outcome would be valuable enough to justify change?

## Interview Guide (draft v0.1)

Grounded in verified findings from SOURCE-REGISTER.md — use these as probes, not a script. Ask open questions first; only reference the regulation/case-study facts below to clarify or challenge a vague answer.

### A. Facility owners, operators, and energy managers (designated facilities, per SRC-023)

1. Does your facility consume more than 180,000 kWh/year? Are you aware of the Energy (Energy Management) Regulations, 2025 designation, and do you have an appointed energy manager and energy management committee?
2. How do you currently track energy use across the grid, solar, generators, and batteries (if present)? Are these on separate dashboards/apps, or one unified view?
3. When you last had an energy audit, what did it identify — and has that finding actually been acted on, or does it recur audit after audit?
4. Have you experienced curtailment, load-matching problems, or situations where your solar/generator output couldn't be fully used or coordinated with grid supply? (Probe drawn directly from SRC-030's confirmed finding.)
5. What operational or financial consequence follows when these systems don't coordinate well (downtime, wasted fuel, wasted solar generation, manual reconciliation effort)?
6. Who would need to approve a new monitoring/control system being connected to your energy assets?

### B. DER installers, integrators, and existing EMS/BMS/SCADA vendors

1. What systems do you typically install or integrate (solar monitoring, BMS, generator controllers, metering)? Do they interoperate with each other and with other vendors' equipment out of the box, or does that require custom work?
2. For facilities you've worked with, what's the most common complaint about visibility or control once multiple systems are installed?
3. Are you aware of Kenyan facilities that upgraded from a fragmented/legacy monitoring setup to a more integrated platform? What specifically was inadequate about the prior setup?
4. In your experience, is the market for facility-level energy coordination already well served by existing platforms, or are there consistent gaps? (Directly tests ASM-005.)

### Guidance for logging responses

- Log every response as its own row in the Discovery Log below, tagged with the correct Evidence Level.
- Do not upgrade an individual observation to "emerging pattern" until at least 3 independent stakeholders in the same category report the same thing.
- If a stakeholder's answer materially confirms or contradicts a Source Register/Evidence Log finding, add a note and cross-reference the relevant SRC-/EV- ID.
