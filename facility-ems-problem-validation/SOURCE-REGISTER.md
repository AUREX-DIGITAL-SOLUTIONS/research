## Source Rules

- Prefer primary and official sources for material factual claims.
- Record the date of publication and the period the data describes.
- Do not generalize one facility, county, vendor, or year into a national claim without evidence.
- Record conflicts, limitations, and uncertainty.
- Link each material conclusion to source IDs in the Evidence Log.
